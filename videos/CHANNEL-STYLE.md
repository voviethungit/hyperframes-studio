# История в Палочках — production direction

The default style for future episodes is a polished 2D explainer cartoon: rounded lightly shaded heads, solid colored bodies, substantial rounded limbs and hands, clean outlines, readable expressive poses and complete illustrated historical environments. Do not return to primitive line-only stick figures, empty beige stages or repeated generic mechanical templates.

Each episode needs a project-specific DESIGN.md and its own palette, setting references, equipment and recurring crew identities. Depth, light, material cues and composition should support the subject. Avoid photorealistic people, plastic-looking 3D, anime, rough doodles and inconsistent drawing styles.

Use imagery and text when they improve understanding. Contextual maps, artifacts, architecture and properly sourced reference images may be shown as deliberate explanatory inserts. Generated reconstructions must not be presented as documentary evidence. Prefer one short correct Russian label, a force arrow, an enlarged mechanical detail or a before/after comparison over dense on-screen text. Do not add automatic subtitles or generated environment writing.

Animation must explain cause, action and result: hands touch handles, ropes remain attached, a hose connects to a real outlet, water goes into its intended reservoir, ladders are carried before being placed, structural pieces fall in the direction of the applied pull, and parked wheels remain stationary. Preserve character colors, positions, equipment and object states across continuations. Establish a new location clearly when changing examples.

The supplied final voice is the immutable master. Align the complete script acoustically, segment by meaning, cover every second, and preserve the original narration without replacing, trimming or time-stretching it. Use 16:9 unless the user specifies another format.

Complete and inspect a representative polished passage internally before rendering a long episode. This is a production quality step, not a requirement to stop for user confirmation. Check every scene composition, sample motion and key mechanism phases, inspect scene boundaries, verify complete export decoding and narration integrity, and state the actual scope of visual QA. Technical export success alone does not establish artistic quality.
