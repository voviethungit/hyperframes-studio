# История в Палочках — Firefighting before engines

## Style Prompt
Polished editorial 2D educational cartoon, rounded off-white shaded heads, black dot eyes and readable expressions, solid colored torsos, dark trousers, substantial rounded limbs and hands. Fine clean charcoal outlines with stronger silhouette edges. Fully illustrated period architecture with windows, timber framing, street furniture, doors, roof tiles, tools and layered depth. Painterly color planes only in background light, never photorealism or plastic 3D. Hero action large and readable. Warm ochre/terracotta historic daylight; cool navy/desaturated teal nights with localized orange firelight.

## Colors
Ink #283338; head #F5F1E5; deep shadows #243341; timber #79513A; plaster #E7CFA5; roofs #A45C49; water #49A9C1; fire core #FFE09A; fire #F18836; smoke #5E626C; shirt anchors teal #3B8991, terracotta #C35D43, ochre #D5A345.

## Typography
PT Sans / DejaVu Sans, bold Russian labels at 40–54px, 1–4 words, maximum one explanatory idea. No automatic subtitles. Text only as clean controlled overlay. No generated signs or writing.

## Motion
Narration aligned from acoustic CTC words. Physically motivated actions, persistent operation time across related shots, scene-specific choreography and camera details. Short cross-dissolves on changes of location, motivated reframing inside operations. First frame complete, no black introduction. No final black tail. No replacement narration. Period gear changes only at explicit narrated development. Flames and smoke reinforce narrative intensity and wind, never conceal action.

## What NOT to Do
No thin line-only bodies, blank beige stages, generic mechanism arrays, cheap repeated bounces, false speaking mouth movements, photoreal people, anime, 3D, modern equipment in old scenes, floating props, ungrounded hands, repetitive copy-paste frames or invented readable environment text. No lip sync. No new voice. No time-stretching.
